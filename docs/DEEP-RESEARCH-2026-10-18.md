# Deep research audit — 18 October 2026

This audit supersedes the earlier sprint report. All amounts are AUD. The catalogue contains 27 configurations across 12 model families, 431 source records, 60 distinct advertised stock observations, 24 professional tests and 53 repair/recall scenarios. Each figure retains its source and applicability notes in the bundled dataset. These counts include unchanged sources rechecked during the audit; they are not all newly discovered sources.

## Ownership figures and methodology

- Reopened the official QLD October 2026 registration/CTP table, duty rates and duty help, plus the government PPSR fee. Existing statutory amounts were correct and retained. No extra GST or duplicate CTP/duty was added.

- The original Tesla RWD retains the requested anonymous public planning defaults: insurance $2,000/year and repair savings $1,800/year. Browser-local quotes retain priority.

## Fuel and electricity assumptions

New-browser defaults now separate 91 RON at $2.21/L and 95 RON at $2.38/L. The regular price is RACQ’s September 2026 Brisbane monthly average, published 7 October. The premium point is explicitly a proxy: $2.210 + (September-quarter PULP95 $2.161 − ULP91 $1.992) = $2.379, rounded. It is not a measured September PULP95 average or a live 18 October pump price. All 11 HEV/PHEV configurations have fuel-grade research. Original Sealion 6, Kluger Hybrid and the new RAV4 use 95 RON; old RAV4 and Outlander permit 91. X-Trail permits 91 but recommends 95. Original Sorento PHEV has conflicting period/current guidance, so its precautionary 95-RON planning grade remains yellow pending the VIN-specific manual. Current-linked Sealion Extended handbook applicability remains explicitly qualified.

Home $0.30/kWh remains illustrative, consistent with the AER’s 27.9744c/kWh GST-inclusive Energex flat standing-offer usage cap for 2026–27. The household’s existing daily supply charge is not added again. ## Calculation consistency

An independent integration check found that saved deal prices affected ownership/depreciation but not the purchase-price score. The score now uses the same purchase value, respects car-specific and legacy shared overrides, and restores the researched value when cleared. Personal prices stay out of shared URLs.

## What cannot become an exact number

Insurance requires real driver/cover quotes. Tyre life, annual repair savings and future depreciation cannot be inferred from a parts listing or a few online reviews. No public Australian model-specific actuarial repair dataset or ten-year resale forecast was verified. Published US-derived battery-price conversions are not Australian installed quotes. The dashboard keeps these estimates yellow, sourced and editable, and does not convert unavailable specifications to invented facts. Ten-year running totals use constant current rates and allowances. Exact child-seat models, simultaneous fit, buckle access and individual VIN recall completion remain inspection tasks.
