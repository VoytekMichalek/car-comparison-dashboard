Tucson A$450 is explicitly a provisional budget because a current VIN quote was unavailable.

[Youi Kia](https://www.youi.com.au/car-insurance/insurance-by-make/kia) and [Hyundai](https://www.youi.com.au/car-insurance/insurance-by-make/hyundai) national means are rounded to A$1,180 Sportage, A$1,190 Tucson and A$1,580 Carnival. They mix years/variants and are not Queensland quotes. The published 80%-within-or-below bands are not confidence intervals.

All three base/S brochures specify 235/65R17. [JAX's current fitted Michelin Primacy 5 offer](https://www.jaxtyres.com.au/tyres/sizes/17-inch/235-65r17) is A$329 each including GST, giving A$1,316 for four. Tyre life remains a yellow assumption; confirm placard and local availability.

Annual repair savings of A$850 Sportage, A$950 Tucson and A$1,300 Carnival are provisional buffers, **not measured annual repair bills**. Warranty, age and diesel complexity inform these editorial choices. No model-specific installed major-component quotes or representative Australian failure-cost dataset was verified. DPF warnings focus on short-trip use and proper diagnosis, not an invented probability.

Original Hyundai five-year warranty is retained. Kia commercial/rental history can impose the 150,000-km limit. Official recall checks include Sportage REC-005699, Tucson REC-006615/REC-006717, and early Carnival latch/fuel-pipe actions. The Carnival power-door campaign is identified as equipment-specific, not automatically attributed to manual-door S.
