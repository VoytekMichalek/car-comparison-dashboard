# Ownership and community-review deep audit — 18 October 2026

This audit covers the dashboard’s 12 families and 27 Australian configuration groups. It reviews warranty applicability, service schedules and public prices, safety campaigns, costly-repair price evidence and owner-rating scope. It does not provide a personal insurance quote, infer a failure probability, or turn a major-repair scenario into an expected annual bill. Insurance, tyres, registration, depreciation, repair savings, professional reviews and scoring code were audited separately by the parent task.

## Material changes

- Added missing official recall context: original Model Y bonnet/tail-lamp/cold-steering software, Juniper window anti-pinch; EV6/Ioniq 5 early park-pawl and selected 2023 driveshaft campaigns; 2025 Kia instrument clusters; RAV4/Kluger combination meters; Kluger second-row recliners and additional earlier seat/bumper/VSC campaigns; 2022 Sorento (P)HEV indicators. Each remedy is recorded at A$0 for an affected VIN.
- Added manufacturer service anchors and configuration distinctions. The 2026 RAV4’s first five standard visits are A$325 each. The older Sorento PHEV has a separate annual planning allowance based on its actual MY22/23 table. Sealion Extended receives an explicitly uncertain analogue rather than inheriting a verified MY24 Dynamic price claim.
- Clarified original versus current warranties, maintenance-history conditions, private/commercial limits, shorter auxiliary/infotainment cover, and component capacity thresholds.
- Corrected the Outlander owner-rating scope: the nominal ZM page includes a visibly older 2021 GSR contribution. The site’s displayed number remains intact; a clean ZM average was not invented.
- Added a tax-included A$1,320 original-FWD Atto 3 compressor **part** listing. A used Model Y HV pack is documented as A$6,500, tax included, **sold out and parts-only**; it does not replace an installed new-pack estimate.
- Made explicit that the Insurance Council’s A$7,500–30,000 battery benchmark converts US research. It is not an Australian installed-price survey. All six BEV full-pack bands remain low-confidence scenarios.

## Recall exclusions and unresolved evidence

Opened official notices identify VIN scope, not failure incidence. The dashboard is not a substitute for the manufacturer’s current VIN campaign check. Additional campaigns may exist, be superseded or depend on equipment/build dates.

- [REC-006742](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006742) is **2026 RAV4 PHEV**, not the HEV configuration, and was excluded.
- [REC-006322](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006322) names Outlander Exceed/Exceed Tourer steering-wheel sensors; not applied to Aspire.
- [REC-005938](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005938) names MY22 Outlander IVI/camera software without explicitly naming PHEV. Exact ZM PHEV applicability was not established; obtain the VIN result rather than asserting inclusion.
- Sorento MQ4 PE lighting/label notices REC-006239/REC-005890 are facelift-specific and were not automatically applied to the older 2021–23 PHEV. The earlier transmission EOP notice REC-005586 was not treated as a proven hybrid six-speed issue.
- No matching Australian 7X or Sealion 6 recall notice was located in the searches performed. This is a search gap, not a categorical “no recalls” claim.
- Direct retrieval of some Mitsubishi HTML/old MY23 PDFs returned 403/failed fetch. The full warranty PDF was downloaded and read; the MY24 purchase-cohort table and current cohort text were retrieved through the official search index. Old MY23 exact-price claims were replaced in the main service basis by the explicitly dated rechecked cohort.
- Coastal Cool Air direct page returned 502 but its current primary search index supplied the May 2026 A$1,000–2,500 installed-compressor guide; the Tesla thermal allowance remains an uncertain adjustment. The NRMA production-host page returned 403; the published official index corroborated class prices and NSW/ACT limits.
- Manufacturer VIN-specific current dealer quotes, original-car warranty amendments, remaining purchased plans, and undisclosed damage/service history cannot be resolved without the actual car. No private VIN search or personal quote was fabricated.
