## Remaining limits

Insurance uses dated, documented profiles and model/class estimates, not a personalised premium. Obtain a quote for your postcode, drivers, excess, agreed value and exact car. Large repair prices are sensitivity ranges, not predicted bills or failure probabilities. Ask the Australian dealer for VIN-specific recall completion and any component-warranty extension in writing; overseas warranty extensions are not assumed.

A ten-year total uses today’s running-cost rates, a repair savings allowance and an uncertain resale scenario. It is not a ten-year quote or inflation forecast. Service caps expire and warranty depends on the car’s age, kilometres, registration date and service history. Your quotes override planning assumptions.

The late-2023 Model Y remains a specific inspection task: verify build month, fitted suspension parts/equipment and remaining cover. No universal Australian changeover date is inferred from overseas reports. Your actual child seats and buckle access remain unconfirmed.
