# Model-by-model audit — 18 October 2026

The current family target is **three restraints: one rear-facing plus two forward-facing seats or boosters**. Exact restraint models and your own installation remain unconfirmed.

**19 families · 35 configurations · 190 item checks · 1,090 source records.** Source records can cite the same URL for separate claims; there are 644 distinct URLs. Each model family has a dedicated research agent and a separate report. Every audit checks pricing, powertrain, family practicality, warranty, servicing, insurance, tyres, repairs, reviews and other ownership considerations. A checked item can still contain an explicitly labelled estimate or unresolved gap. These checks are not a percentage of facts proven correct.

## What changed

- Fit scores now distinguish the demonstrated arrangement, booster buckle access, restraint/front-seat clearance and loading/row access. Unverified criteria add no score or weight; they reduce evidence influence. Related trims and generations receive less influence than a matched configuration. No established mixed arrangement means no family-fit score. Personal confirmation remains browser-local.
- Original Model Y RWD scores 8.6/10 for published family practicality versus 6.4/10 for the previous-generation RAV4 GXL's related tests. This reflects the documented clearance and arrangement constraints, not a controlled head-to-head test or a promise that your seats fit. Booster buckle access remains unverified for both.
- Matched observed EV energy use is displayed separately from manufacturer claims. Where the measurement boundary is known, it informs the ownership energy budget. AAA mains-recharge figures already include charging losses; they are not adjusted a second time. Personal cost overrides still take precedence.
- Asking samples distinguish physical cars, fees, kilometres, state and year. The price caption now states the years actually priced, avoiding the implication that an older used-car sample prices every year in a configuration group. Blocked, sold, duplicate and mismatched stock is excluded where identified.
- Original warranty and service schedules are separated from later model offers. Examples include Ioniq 5 low-conductivity coolant, original EV6 coolant intervals, Sorento PHEV inverter coolant and Subaru period-specific handbooks. Current warranty extensions require the actual car's eligibility.
- Repair reserves remain low-confidence savings contributions. Public parts prices, installed repair offers, free VIN-specific recall remedies, inspection defects and roadside breakdowns establish different things. No representative Australian annual failure-cost dataset was found that justifies treating those reserves as predicted bills.
- Owner aggregates are screened for generation, trim, drivetrain and contributor type. Mixed or small samples have less influence. An unusably contaminated aggregate is withheld. Professional reviews remain separate and retain their original rating scales.

## Remaining limits

Try your exact three restraints in the exact vehicle, including secure installation, buckle access, front-seat position and third-row access with restraints occupied. A successful harnessed-seat test does not establish easy booster buckling. ANCAP installation assessments test selected restraints individually; they do not prove simultaneous three-across fit. A reviewer moving a seat with a restraint installed does not override the vehicle manual.

Insurance figures are public-cohort or analyst planning budgets, not household quotes. Tyre life, future resale and repair funds remain assumptions. Advertised prices are dated asking evidence, not sale prices or valuations. Scope gaps are retained for untested trims, material year changes and unverified charging/consumption figures. No physical car or restraint installation was inspected during this desk research.
