Row-three centre is not recommended for restraints because it lacks the requisite anchors. ANCAP's individual-seat fit limitations remain visible; ANCAP expressly says its installation tests do not establish simultaneous multi-seat fit.

## Servicing and warranty

The [Kia capped-service menu](https://dashboard.kia.com.au/kia/cps/kia/20170105v1_flat.html) verifies MY21/22 diesel seven-visit totals A$3,767 and MY23 A$3,816. The existing A$600/year provision remains conservative relative to those normal-schedule averages, but **does not fully price severe use or work after 105,000 km**. A high-km used car needs its next actual service priced rather than a new-car first-seven average.

The correct Australian MY22 manual, pp 8-15–18, explicitly labels **Smartstream D2.2 timing belt**, separately from drive belts: inspect at **120,000 km**; replace timing-belt system including water pump, tensioner and idler at **240,000 km**. This is not an assumption imported from the petrol V6. It also specifies first coolant replacement **210,000 km/120 months**, then **30,000 km/24 months**, brake fluid **24 months**, fuel-filter cartridge **60,000 km/48 months** under normal conditions. Repeated short trips under 8 km, heavy traffic and stop-start use are among severe conditions: diesel engine oil/filter then requires **7,500 km/6 months**. Obtain a local VIN-specific quote for extra visits. Costs were not invented for unquoted work.

The [current Kia warranty terms](https://www.kia.com/content/dam/kwcms/au/en/files/service/Kia-Warranty-terms-and-conditions.pdf), which expressly describe vehicles sold from October 2014, confirm transfer and usage conditions. Retain the original delivery-date contract because the public PDF was revised in 2026. Shorter component terms and wear/DPF maintenance exclusions are visible in the dashboard. A dealer-backed statutory or manufacturing-defect remedy is not the same as buying a scheduled service or replacing a worn item. Prior rental/rideshare history particularly matters to a used Carnival.

## Repairs and expensive exposure

A better matched public price anchor was found at [Total Parts Plus, Brisbane](https://www.totalpartsplus.com.au/car/2021-kia-carnival/): the inventory specifies KA4 D4HE diesel and lists used DPF **A$660**, eight-speed diesel gearbox **A$2,500**, engine **A$7,250**. The page's introductory 2017 prose is inconsistent with its 2021 title; the narrated donor description and individual part fitment explicitly say KA4/D4HE. Use only those clearly matched inventory lines. They are **used component asks**, not fitted new-part repair quotes; the dashboard leaves risk low/high repair totals unknown and explains labour, diagnosis, consumables and GST uncertainty. Their availability does not establish a common failure.

Official recall records read:

- [REC-005019](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005019), selected early right sliding-door latches.
- [REC-001615](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-001615), selected early fuel supply pipes.
- [REC-005685](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005685), powered sliding-door warning software: manual S equipment must be distinguished.
- [REC-006644](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006644), **3.5-litre V6 only**: do not apply its 2026 fuel-pipe issue to the 2.2 diesel.
- [REC-006557](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006557), selected 2025 cluster vehicles: not a blanket original S issue.

Applicable recall work is free and VIN-specific. No representative Australian annual paid-repair dataset, installed KA4 engine/gearbox quote, or reliable failure probability was verified. The A$1,300 [A$500–3,500] annual repair fund therefore stays a clearly labelled cash provision. Do not multiply recycler price by an invented failure rate, or treat every warranty repair story as a bill paid by the owner. A major failure can exceed the fund.
