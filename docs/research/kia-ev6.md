# Kia EV6 deep audit — 18 October 2026

Configuration: `ev6-air-2022`, original Australian Air RWD, 77.4 kWh, grouped 2022–24. This audit does not silently extend findings to the 84 kWh facelift or GT.

The data pack is `src/data/research/kia-ev6.json`. All ten audit categories are present. The important addition is exact-variant AAA evidence, rather than more generic EV assumptions.

## Family fit and score distinction

BabyDrive demonstrated **two rear-facing InfaSecure seats and a centre Aspire More** in a GT-Line RWD, reporting difficult centre-buckle access and front clearance for a 186 cm occupant. That supports longitudinal room, with a meaningful three-across compromise. It does not demonstrate every Air arrangement or the household’s **one rear-facing plus two forward-facing/booster** combination.

An independent **Air** family test also describes useful front clearance but favours two restraints and warns three require narrow seats. It flags awkward manual door handles. ANCAP’s individual installation assessment documents a centre-booster failure; its matrix is not a simultaneous three-seat test.

The proposed assessment therefore separates **arrangement: workable; buckles: restricted; space: good; access: workable**, with overall scope **related**. This is more useful than a blanket “three fit” score. Air anchors and rear vents are now manufacturer-confirmed instead of left as trim uncertainties. Your exact fit remains pending.

Sources: [BabyDrive GT-Line test](https://babydrive.com.au/reviews/electric-cars/2022-kia-ev6-gt-line-rwd/), [CarsGuide Air family test](https://www.carsguide.com.au/family/kia-ev6-air-92472), [ANCAP report](https://cdn.ancap.com.au/app/public/assets/f28a0b6aac3ac9eb1e3aabea1c85a2ed2b9c7b91/original.pdf?1663120918=).

## Limits and follow-up

The old MY22 manual URL is dead and web retrieval of the new PDF failed. Kia’s public manual index returned the current document paths; both PDFs were downloaded directly and text-extracted successfully. The same method read the archived MY22 manufacturer brochure.

The previously reported Australian 15-year/300,000-km ICCU extension remains a **reported** benefit requiring written VIN-specific confirmation. Drive’s underlying article could not be reopened, and the current public warranty PDF does not spell it out. Overseas extension letters cannot establish Australian entitlement.

The existing paid ICCU and complete-battery stress ranges remain labelled foreign/general proxies, not locally validated installed quotes or annual averages. The US OEM parts page and Insurance Council report were reopened; neither closes that local quote gap. Ordinary scheduled fluids are separate from a repair savings fund. A$1,000 is therefore retained as an editable allowance, not presented as a measured EV6 failure bill.

## Integration facing-direction recheck

The published photograph and written trial do not establish the household’s exact one-rear-facing plus two-forward-facing/booster mix. Keep the positive different-arrangement evidence and clearance observations, but withhold the family-fit score rather than infer a fit. This is an evidence gap, not a demonstrated failure.
