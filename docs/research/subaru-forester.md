# Subaru Forester audit — 18 October 2026

Covers `forester-25il-2021` (S5 petrol 2.5i-L, MY21–24) and `forester-awd-petrol-2025` (S6 MY26 petrol base, sold from June 2025). S5 and S6 evidence is kept separate. The family target is **one rear-facing restraint plus two forward-facing seats or boosters**; actual restraint models remain unspecified.

- Stronger S5 evidence was missing: BabyDrive’s **2024 Sport** tested the requested facing mix, with a capsule behind a 184 cm driver. This is related-grade evidence for the 2.5i-L, including facelift years. It replaces reliance solely on the older Premium test, which used two rear-facing restraints. Neither generation has verified daily booster-buckle access with the requested combination. ## Important limits and scoring

A **0.5 S5 owner-review scope factor** is defensible: same broad generation, but mixed years/grades/engines and imperfect publisher classification. Self-selected reviews do not measure fault incidence. S6 owner aggregate should have zero influence until an applicable cohort exists. The 2.5i-L-only subpage has fewer observations and obvious classification problems; choosing its higher number would not make the evidence stronger.

The rear-facing result materially improves the S5 arrangement assessment. It does not erase the centre-position restrictions, unknown booster access or the need to install the household’s seats independently. No body-width calculation is used as proof. Unknown criteria carry no scoring influence.

Australian S5 handbook says normal-use CVT oil is inspected, with extreme-condition servicing referred to the workshop manual. Do not invent a universal 60,000 km replacement rule from overseas forum advice. Older used cars may have costly services due; ask for the next two itemised service quotes and the completed differential-fluid/coolant/spark-plug records relevant to their VIN and mileage. The obsolete base-registry `3194...` handbook was not used for period-specific terms; its source reference was removed from this pack’s ownership/repair-reserve updates. The [September 2023 SA1196 handbook](https://docs.subaru.com.au/SA1196-12-Month-12500-Kilometre-Service-Interval-Warranty-and-Service-Handbook.pdf) independently confirms MY21 onward S5 schedule and battery exclusions. The generic 2024 15,000 km handbook does not list Forester applicability, so its schedule was **not** assigned to S6.

The Subaru of America TCV bulletin supports an early-S5 inspection lead and describes an improved part; its 15-year/150,000-mile extension is not verified Australian entitlement. Australian genuine-parts catalogue fitment is broad, and a complete installed price was not published in readable content. Forum cost anecdotes were searched but not promoted to an Australian repair average or installed quotation. Ask Subaru about goodwill before paying. Historic EJ-headgasket issues, old-generation CVT campaigns, US sunroof/label campaigns, and the new hybrid transaxle campaign were not assigned to these petrol configurations.
