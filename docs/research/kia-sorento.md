# Kia Sorento deep audit — 18 October 2026

Scope: original Australian GT-Line PHEV AWD (2021–2023) and facelift MY26 GT-Line HEV AWD. These are separate configurations. The target is three restraints including one rear-facing, with two forward-facing seats or boosters. Your exact restraints still need a fit check.

## Main findings

The original PHEV has direct, named-restraint evidence for the mixed target and useful front-seat clearance. The facelift HEV has strong related-year evidence; it should not receive the same evidence confidence as a direct MY26 test. Neither test establishes daily booster-buckle reach for your seats.

The current QLD MY23 PHEV advertisement is now **A$49,990 drive-away**, down from the stored A$51,990. Three verified cars still produce a **A$52,499 median**, with a **A$49,990–54,990** span. A supposedly cheaper Mornington car is sold and is also cross-posted with conflicting HEV/PHEV labels; it is excluded.

The original Australian MY23 manual permits **91–94 RON**, while recommending 95 for best performance. Together with the period brochure and period road test, this supports **91 RON planning for the original PHEV**, with medium confidence across the grouped years. Later facelift PHEV guidance must not be applied automatically. Check the actual fuel flap, especially on 2021–2022 cars.

## Maintenance detail worth inspecting

The Australian MY23 schedule, printed pp 8-10–12, identifies PHEV inverter coolant at **60,000 km/36 months**, HSG belt at **100,000 km/48 months**, brake fluid at **20,000 km/24 months**, and spark plugs at **70,000 km**. Check invoices. The applicable severe-use schedule can reduce oil servicing to **5,000 km/6 months**, and the HSG belt to **50,000 km/24 months**. Do not charge normal scheduled work again as a repair reserve expense.

The MY26 AU/NZ schedule, printed pp 9-9–11, retains the normal oil and belt intervals but places initial HEV engine/inverter coolant at **180,000 km/120 months**. It is not the PHEV’s three-year inverter coolant schedule. Repeated short trips and stop-start conditions appear in the severe-use criteria; obtain a schedule for the actual family usage.
