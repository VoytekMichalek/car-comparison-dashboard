Checked **18 October 2026**. Australian dollars, annual comprehensive cover; CTP remains separate. No quotes were submitted and no personal insurance inputs were changed.

The $1,700 recommendations for the unquoted PHEV families are explicitly **class proxies**, rounded from the published $1,709 benchmark. They are not model means. Choosing Youi's mixed Sorento mean instead would produce about $1,400, but silently treating its older petrol/diesel/hybrid mixture as current GT-Line hybrid evidence would overstate the match. An exact household quote should replace either proxy.

No generation uplift, PHEV premium multiplier or reduction proportional to used purchase price is supported by these sources. The updated 22.7-kWh Outlander, original 20-kWh Aspire, old Sorento PHEV and newer Sorento HEV therefore retain explicit configuration gaps rather than invented individual precision.

## Brisbane quote-study benchmark

[Compare the Market, published 9 April 2026](https://www.comparethemarket.com.au/news/ev-insurance-up-in-12-months/) reports March 2026 annual means: **RAV4 GX 2WD HEV $1,844**, **Sealion 6 Essential $1,821**, ten-model HEV/PHEV mean **$1,709**. Outlander, X-Trail, Kluger and Sorento are not individual quoted models.

The study covered **11 insurers / six underwriters**, with availability varying by car; exact per-model quote counts are not disclosed. Reference: 34-year-old man, Alderley Brisbane 4051, market value, approximately $900 excess, clean history, garaged, up to 15,000 km/year, private/commuting use, next-day **5 March 2026** commencement. It averages available quotes, not policies customers chose. Its separate 908-person survey is **not the premium-study sample size**.

Existing source ID: `budget-insurance-study`, plus the family-specific study aliases. The insurer mean does not identify the most competitive suitable policy for this family.

## Outlander PHEV evidence and exclusions

- [The Car Guy's original 22 May 2024 Exceed Tourer PHEV review](https://thecarguy.com.au/review-mitsubishi-outlander-exceed-tourer-phev-may-2024/) publishes annual comprehensive comparisons **Suncorp $1,541 / RACQ $1,800** for a new original-generation 20-kWh PHEV. Two insurer figures, not two household samples. Driver age, exact postcode, insured-value basis, excess and optional-cover equivalence are not specified in that article. The author's other articles use personal Queensland details, but those must not be imported into this quote without evidence. Useful dated powertrain/generation cross-check; not an Aspire or 2026 renewal quote. Suggested source ID: `insurance-outlander-phev-carguy-2024`.
- [RAC WA 2024 summary PDF](https://rac.com.au/-/media/files/rac-website/car-and-motoring/running-costs/024-car-running-costs_final2.pdf) lists Outlander PHEV Aspire, but **$200.48 is monthly total standing cost**, not insurance. Its table has no separate comprehensive premium. Do not multiply that column by twelve and label it insurance.
- [RACQ 2025 running-cost study](https://media.racq.com.au/car/research/car-running-costs/running-costs-2025) also combines registration and insurance. Its listed Outlander is **petrol ES**; no PHEV-specific premium can be extracted directly. No subtraction from the current QLD rego default was used.
- Search located a syndicated 2025 GSR article claiming $2,015 (plus Sorento PHEV $2,074 and Sealion 6 $1,991, Chatswood 35-year-old male). The original Drive page was unavailable for verification. **These figures are not recommended as new primary dataset anchors.**
- US/UK/Canadian premiums, predecessor Outlander quotes and old 2018 Sorento prices were excluded from current Australian defaults.

The available evidence does not establish a universal $1,900 PHEV premium or that $1,100 is impossible. The old $1,300 lower bound was too narrow to communicate the household uncertainty. Replace the unsupported SUV uplift, widen the budget sensitivity and keep the actual quote override prominent.
