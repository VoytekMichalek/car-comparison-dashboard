# Nissan X-Trail e-POWER audit

Checked 18 October 2026. Configurations: `xtrail-ti-epower-awd` (now pre-MY26, 2023–25) and `xtrail-stl-epower-fwd-2026` (new MY26 FWD). The target is three restraints including one rear-facing; seat models are still unspecified.

The important improvement is distinguishing a demonstrated mixed three-seat arrangement from an unrestricted fit claim. The new FWD has related evidence rather than its own three-seat test. MY26 Ti AWD is now an explicit coverage gap rather than being combined with older dimensions, equipment and servicing.

Fuel budgets now use observed evidence separately from official claims. Old Ti AWD planning consumption is **6.7 L/100 km**, with **5.8–8.5** sensitivity: related AAA Ti-L test 6.7; exact Ti long-term result 7.4 with highway bias. The latter is recorded as observed consumption with its scope. FWD planning consumption is **6.3 L/100 km**, with assumed **5.4–8.0** sensitivity, informed by two launch trip-computer results (6.0 and 6.5). These routes are not a representative Australian household sample. Fuel-grade choice remains separate from consumption.

For seating scores, the arrangement is **workable**, space/access positive where supported, and buckles **unknown**. Related-trim evidence must retain lower influence. ANCAP tested individual positions, not simultaneous combinations. Its tested centre boosters failed; a different BabyDrive combination can still fit. A centre forward-facing harnessed seat with an outboard rear-facing restraint and outboard booster deserves a physical trial. This audit does not certify that arrangement for the family's own seats.

Public Australian owner's-manual access was not located: Nissan Australia's support page directs owners to a service adviser. UK/Indian manual instructions and overseas generator/engine anecdotes were not imported as Australian evidence. No empirical annual repair average, assured future resale or post-five-visit service cap was found. Current NissanConnect is complimentary for three years; future subscription pricing is unspecified.

## Later integration: used-car warranty deadline

The petrol researcher located [Nissan’s 8 September 2025 release](https://australia.nissannews.com/en-AU/releases/limited-time-remains-for-eligible-customers-to-unlock-australias-only-10-year300000km-warranty-extension). Root read it and the current warranty terms section 3(e). The advertised 2021–24 re-entry health-check deadline was 31 December 2025, now past. Nissan retains discretionary written approval under current terms; this does not establish automatic new eligibility for a used car in October 2026. Verify this e-Power VIN’s original cover, service history and written extension status.
