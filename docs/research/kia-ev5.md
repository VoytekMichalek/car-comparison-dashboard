# Kia EV5 deep audit — 18 October 2026

Audited both Air configurations: Standard Range and Long Range, Australian 2024–2026 grouped entry. This pass read maker MY25/MY26 sheets, the 740-page MY25 manual's relevant sections, ANCAP applicability and restraint-installation report, family test, six individual clean advertisements plus one conflicting seller page, warranty/service terms, insurance/tyre retail evidence and original professional/owner posts.

## Material changes

- Original MY25 Australian specification was located on an Australian dealer document host. It matches MY26 battery, range, rated consumption, AC rates, towing and advertised charging times. Earlier-build uncertainty can be removed for these verified items; the sheets do not supply vehicle peak DC intake, so that remains blank. A 350 kW charger claim is not a 350 kW car.
- Changed the Air family label to **related-trim tested** rather than simply unverified. The independent three-seat result is for Earth AWD and is constrained; exact Air fit and three-booster buckle access remain unknown. The four separate fit criteria carry that distinction. The current family target is one rear-facing plus two forward-facing seats/boosters. Separate rear-facing clearance evidence is positive, but the article does not establish the exact full mix. This does not warrant an unconstrained high three-across score or an automatic mixed-setup failure.
- Added the ANCAP outer-position Type A capsule **seatbelt installation restriction**. This is separate from the user's forward-facing requirements, and from simultaneous three-across. ANCAP explicitly says multiple restraints were not tested.
- Added the MY25 maintenance schedule: low-conductivity coolant every 36 months/60,000 km; AU/NZ brake fluid every 24 months/30,000 km. Standard coolant has a different initial 120-month/180,000-km interval. This is a material used-car due-service check. Do not copy an overseas schedule without its AU/NZ footnotes.
- Added Air LR **16.7 kWh/100 km indicated** road-test consumption, kept separate from **18.0 WLTP**. The CarExpert week covered over 250 km; its over-520-km range is extrapolation, so no observedRange was created.
- Added original Carsales Air LR launch review **78/100 → 7.8/10**, tested October 2024. The existing MY26 review remains separately scoped; reports about Earth/GT-Line chassis behaviour are not Air tests.
- Corrected stock T4746 exterior colour to Clear White. T4754 dealer prose incorrectly calls the BEV a self-charging hybrid; the verified specification takes precedence.

## Warranty, faults and uncertainty

Kia warranty terms explicitly separate private vehicle seven-year/unlimited-km cover, prior commercial-use limits, seven-year/150,000-km HV components, pre-MY27 battery capacity to 70%, two-year 12V and three-year head unit. Verify original commencement and all prior use. Do not give these 2024–26 cars MY27's longer battery term.

REC-006557 was **published 6 March 2026** for selected **2025** VINs. It is a free cluster software campaign, not a priced owner-paid repair or proof that every EV5 is affected.

First-person Australian comments describe a 12V roadside replacement, another owner's pending front-motor replacement, and a battery replaced under warranty in around three weeks. They are unverified self-reports without invoices or an exposure denominator. A much-discussed Canadian main post is not an Australian repair-duration or failure-frequency estimate. No Australian EV5-specific paid battery/drive-unit installed quote or representative annual repair average was found. **A$650/year remains an explicitly low-confidence savings reserve**, not an empirical repair cost; nothing in these anecdotes justifies a precise change. Generic battery/suspension stress-case amounts remain estimates and must not be presented as EV5 invoices. Do not transfer EV6/800V ICCU recall rates to EV5's different system.

Owner aggregate remains **5/5, n=1**, tagged GT-Line and chiefly a dealership delivery review. It provides virtually no Air reliability information. Professional review scores are not owner-reliability evidence.

QLD electric registration + private Class 1 CTP currently **A$782.15–798.15/year**. Future renewal rates, decade-long resale and repairs remain projections. Towing is strongly configuration-specific: SR 300 kg braked/50 kg ball versus LR 1,250 kg/100 kg ball. Check payload with occupants/restraints/luggage. Air's manual tailgate, no spare and lack of top-grade surround-view camera matter when comparing Earth test equipment.
