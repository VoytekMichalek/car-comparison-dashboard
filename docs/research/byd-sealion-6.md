# BYD Sealion 6 deep audit

Checked 18 October 2026. Configurations: `sealion6-dynamic-183` (original Dynamic FWD, 18.3 kWh) and `sealion6-dynamic-extended` (Dynamic Extended FWD, 26.6 kWh). This report audits the existing entries; Essential, Premium AWD and the different Sealion 7/8 are not treated as the same configuration.

The useful correction is warranty coverage: the previous repair-reserve explanation cited shorter component cover that was reportedly removed retrospectively in April 2025. The new family requirement remains unproven for this model: **one rear-facing restraint plus two forward-facing seats or boosters**. Anchors alone do not establish this fit.

## Family fit and why the score should remain uncertain

The Australian handbook and ANCAP technical report verify two rear outboard ISOFIX positions and three rear top tethers. ANCAP explicitly tests restraints separately and warns that simultaneous installations have not been assessed. Its 12/12 installation result is therefore not a three-across result.

CarExpert's 2025 Premium AWD test says three child seats fit tightly. It supplies no restraint models, facing directions or positions, and no booster-buckle observation. Its adult legroom observation does not measure usable front-adult space with a rear-facing seat installed. That is useful related evidence, but insufficient to label the household arrangement demonstrated. Searches for BabyDrive Sealion 6 tests instead returned Sealion **7** and **8**, which cannot be transferred.

Both configuration assessments therefore mark arrangement, buckles, rear-facing/front-seat space and loading access **unknown**, with zero scoring influence. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that the seats fail to fit. Fit the actual three seats before buying. The handbook also makes the cargo-cover removal/top-tether access worth checking in practice.

## Observed energy and variant boundaries

CarExpert retrospectively reports approximately **90 km EV driving in the previous base FWD**. Added this as a separately labelled observed-range fact, with route/weather/build details unknown. It also describes 4.9 L/100 km after an initial 100 km, but the retrospective test method is not complete enough to replace the official depleted figure. The same article's 80 km/5.3 L results belong to Premium AWD and are not transferred.

Driving Enthusiast's original Dynamic 5.1 L/100 km involved charging every other day; the August 2026 Essential review's 1.5 L/100 km also does not establish depleted fuel use. Neither can be used as the petrol-only leg of the household calculator. Official 16.9/14.7 kWh figures do not resolve wall versus battery or weighted versus electric-distance boundaries, so the separate labelled 19 kWh/100 km electric-use planning estimate remains. Charging-loss assumptions are still applied once. No mathematical charging time is presented as a tested claim.

## Recall and market limitations

The official BYD VIN recall checker was read. No actual inspected VIN was supplied to check all outstanding campaigns. A targeted government search found no verified Sealion 6 campaign in this pass; this does **not** mean a recall-free car. A generic repair-price page surfaced Atto 3's REC-005579; the official record identifies Atto 3, so that recall is not assigned to Sealion 6. Owner use of the word “recall” is not confirmation of an official safety campaign.

Cars24's current model page no longer lists its original 23,990 km Dynamic; it is excluded. The 3,189 km Azure Blue H30165 Carsales record remains stale against a sold cross-post already documented in the dataset; it stays excluded. Exact new stock observations are verified records, not three independent sellers or verified build dates. Original Dynamic, later Essential, Extended and AWD asking prices are not pooled.

No empirical annual repair average was found. Public generic cost pages listing irrelevant diesel jobs were not adopted as model-specific quotations. Existing battery/inverter analogue donor pages were re-read, but do not become compatible BYD repair quotes or evidence of failure frequency.
