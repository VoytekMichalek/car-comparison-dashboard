## Family-fit interpretation

The [BabyDrive Sport trial](https://babydrive.com.au/reviews/suvs/all-new-2021-subaru-outback-sport/) is related-grade evidence. Restraint models are unnamed. The article gives conflicting front-passenger heights, 182 and 184 cm; the pack retains 182 conservatively and discloses the discrepancy. The roof-mounted centre belt complicated installation. Loading access was satisfactory.

The [ANCAP installation chart](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.ancap.com.au/app/public/assets/bbdcc0569192c378e9e67d4a3836e424eb2d6f8b/original.pdf), downloaded and rendered locally because text extraction loses the colours, marks selected harnessed rear restraints green and both centre boosters red. ANCAP assesses positions separately and expressly does not establish multiple-seat fit.

The evidence rubric is **arrangement workable; space good; access good; daily booster buckles unknown**. Related scope and the missing buckle assessment reduce influence. The resulting raw practicality score is 7.2/10 under the shared rubric; no category weights were changed to force a ranking. The exact [base family test](https://www.carsguide.com.au/family/subaru-outback-awd-82821) supports doors, vents and 522 L VDA cargo practicality. Its automatic specifications box incorrectly describes a 2.0D; the prose clearly identifies the new 2.5 petrol base, so that box was not imported.

## Warranty, services and repairs

The correct manufacturer [MY21-onward non-XT handbook](https://docs.subaru.com.au/SA1196-12-Month-12500-Kilometre-Service-Interval-Warranty-and-Service-Handbook.pdf) is the September 2023 SA1196 edition, linked by [Subaru’s current warranty page](https://www.subaru.com.au/new-vehicle-warranty). The baseline 3194 handbook includes an obsolete six-month Outback schedule and should not establish BT servicing. The newer guide lists **12 months/12,500 km**, coolant at **75,000 km/72 months**, differential oils at **50,000 km/48 months**, plugs and fuel filter at **100,000 km/96 months**, and brake fluid every **25,000 km/24 months**. CVT fluid is inspected; normal-use replacement is not scheduled, with extreme-condition guidance deferred to the workshop manual. Verify the original car’s book and usage.

Initial caps are year-specific: [MY21](https://docs.subaru.com.au/MY21_Outback_Capped_Price_Servicing.pdf) A$344.96, 594.80, 351.14, 801.42, 357.56; [MY25](https://docs.subaru.com.au/MY25-Subaru-Outback-(excluding-XT)-Capped-Price-Servicing.pdf) A$373.87, 649.48, 382.32, 907.96, 402.04, including GST. At 15,000 km/year the distance-scaled references are about A$588/A$652, before later used-car work. The **A$750/year service budget** remains a low-confidence used-car allowance, not a current capped quote.

Five-year unlimited-kilometre private-use cover transfers with the car subject to terms; 12 V batteries have two-year written cover and ordinary suspension wear is excluded. Dealer extensions, official recall remedies and consumer guarantees are separate. Used-car warranty does not restart.

Recall sources checked:

- [REC-001227 / SRT306](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-001227) and [REC-005005 / SRT315](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005005): selected MY21 unwanted braking/software.
- [REC-005830 / SRT351](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005830): 2,610 selected MY21 Outbacks, inhibitor switch affecting reversing lamps/camera; not a claim that the gearbox needs replacement.
- [REC-005831 / SRT-352](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005831): selected MY23 driveshaft defect; check VIN list and completion.

The [US TCV bulletin](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2024/MC-10253930-0001.pdf) is a valid early-car inspection lead, not an Australian warranty entitlement, annual failure rate or fitted Australian quote. Public owner complaints about bushes/batteries support inspection questions, not incidence or averaged dollars. No representative Australian paid-repair average, installed BT CVT quote or installed BT TCV quote was verified. **A$1,100/year repair reserve (A$500–2,400)** therefore stays an explicitly uncertain savings allowance; it excludes services, tyres and insured collisions. A large repair can exceed it.
