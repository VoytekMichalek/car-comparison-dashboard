# Toyota Kluger Hybrid GX eFour AWD audit

Checked 18 October 2026. Configuration: `kluger-gx-hev-awd`, Australian XU70, 2021–26. Target: three restraints including a rear-facing seat and two forward-facing seats or boosters. This audit does not confirm the household’s unspecified seats.

## Material findings

- The [BabyDrive 2021 Grande test](https://babydrive.com.au/reviews/suvs/2021-toyota-kluger-hybrid-grande/) names an Aspire More booster and two Attain More restraints, with forward- and rear-facing installations. It reports clearance for a 180 cm driver ahead of the rear-facing Attain More, even with a 162 cm occupant in row 3. This is stronger evidence than merely counting anchors. It remains related-grade evidence for GX; the exact one-rear/two-forward positions are not fully mapped in its text.
- The [ANCAP technical report](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.ancap.com.au/app/public/assets/534362bf4ca200cb445ddb1b17b2fd7c1bde22c0/original.pdf), pp. 3–4, reports a rear-facing convertible that could not be correctly installed with outboard ISOFIX, and care needed for an outboard belt-installed capsule. Its position tests are separate installations, not three-across tests. This prevents treating the positive family test as universal compatibility.
- Both the [original hybrid owner manual](https://toyotamanuals.com.au/docs/kluger-hybrid-owners-manual-oct-21-current/) and [October 2022-on manual](https://toyotamanuals.com.au/docs/kluger-hybrid-owners-manual-oct-22-current/), child-restraint chapters, warn against adjusting the vehicle seat after securing a restraint. The existing entry’s suggestion to slide an ISOFIX-occupied seat for third-row entry was too permissive. The published observation is now explicitly separated from the manual warning.
- No row-3 top tether or ISOFIX is provided. ANCAP recommends against child-restraint installation there. BabyDrive’s particular untethered Versatile Folding Booster experiment is not a general extra child-seat position, so it is no longer presented as a solution for the household’s arrangement.

The fit assessment is **arrangement workable, buckles unknown, space good, access workable**, with related scope. Unknown buckles should contribute no score. The arrangement assessment reflects the positive named test and the documented compatibility exception; it is not adjusted to produce a desired comparison ranking.

## Warranty, service and age

The [Australian 2021 launch](https://www.toyota.com.au/discover/news/all-new-toyota-kluger-arrives-in-australia) verifies original five-year unlimited-km private cover, conditional seven-year engine/driveline cover, and historical A$250 normal scheduled service visits under the five-year/75,000 km entitlement. That clock does not restart at a used sale. The current [warranty page](https://www.toyota.com.au/owners/warranty) and [full April 2026 terms](https://www.toyota.com.au/-/media/project/toyota/toyota/owners/warranty/20260401_toyota-warranty-advantage_tandcs_t2026-022197.pdf?rev=a9c6245447e84c2fa4c0cff88b0c50c5) distinguish the annual traction-battery health-check extension to ten years from seven-year engine/electric-motor/driveline cover. HEV normal capacity loss is excluded; the 70% capacity promise is BEV-only. Prior commercial use and original delivery dates matter. Check the original book and Toyota’s eligibility record.

Retain **A$500/year servicing**, assumed **A$300–700**, for an aged used purchase. The [service-pricing terms](https://www.toyota.com.au/-/media/toyota/main-site/page-data/service/service_pricing_terms_and_conditions_20250519-v3.pdf?rev=bbd159226ba049d9ad7abe267a1746b4) include scheduled parts, labour and fluids, but exclude extra severe-use/wear items. No VIN-specific post-cap quote or dependable ten-year average was obtained. Public detailed service-book links now redirect to login/unavailable; exact spark-plug, coolant and brake-fluid due services therefore remain an inspection task, not invented intervals. The owner manual specifically requires traction-battery cooling intake checks and cleaning; a clogged intake/warning should be investigated.

## Official recall review

- [REC-006562 / 6GG11](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006562): row-2 recliner. [Toyota’s 24 September 2026 expansion](https://pressroom.toyota.com.au/PressRelease?pr-code=65c7365b-3276-4872-8a7a-7fc527870b08) extends selected production through **10 June 2025**, with **46,467** Australian vehicles; free return-spring remedy.
- [REC-006125](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006125): separate row-2 seat-frame protector, selected 2021–24.
- [March–July 2023 spiral-cable campaign](https://www.toyota.com.au/discover/news/toyota-australia-recalls-kluger-vehicles-due-to-spiral-cable-assembly): driver-airbag circuit.
- [REC-006390](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006390): selected meter display software. Published notice still anticipates Q4 2025 software; obtain present VIN remedy status rather than assuming completion.
- [REC-005459](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005459): selected 2021 front-seat frames.
- [REC-005813](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-005813): selected 2021–23 lower-bumper fasteners.
- [Toyota VSC campaign](https://www.toyota.com.au/discover/news/toyota-australia-recalls-vehicles-due-to-vehicle-stability-control-system-software): selected early hybrid builds, software reset condition.

These campaigns are VIN-specific free remedies, not seven annual owner-paid repair bills. Their existence cannot be converted into an annual failure probability.

Exact production changeover remains to be checked; early used listings cannot inherit later equipment.
