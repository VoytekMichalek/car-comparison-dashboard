# Budget replacement tyre prices: compact SUVs

Implementation decisions and final figures: [consolidated update](BUDGET-TYRES-UPDATE-2026-10-18.md). The integrated dashboard uses a common 40,000 km life assumption with 25,000–60,000 km sensitivity; earlier recommendations below are research notes, not the final defaults.

Checked **18 October 2026**. Prices are Australian dollars per tyre unless stated. This is a retail price audit, not a comparative braking or durability test. It supports budgeting for compatible aftermarket replacements rather than assuming manufacturer-branded, acoustic or EV-specific tyres are compulsory.

## Recommended planning anchors

Use the ordinary JAX displayed fitted prices below, without a temporary promotion. JAX includes fitting, balancing, a tubeless valve, waste-tyre management, shipping to the selected store and GST. Availability is conditional on selecting a store: these are Australian advertised prices, **not confirmed Brisbane stock or a personalised Queensland quote**. Wheel alignment is additional. The actual tyre placard and wheel package still govern suitability.

The sensitivity bounds and lifespans are editable planning assumptions, not observed quotations at both ends or model-specific wear studies. Annual cost = four-tyre set price × annual km ÷ assumed tyre life. Changing the replacement brand does not establish a longer life, identical efficiency or equal wet braking. Retain the existing lifespan uncertainty rather than inventing new wear evidence.

These anchors cut the previous premium defaults substantially: original/EVO Atto 3 $1,276 → $660/$716 per set; Essential $1,140 → $580; EV5/older RAV4/Outback $1,236 → $756. Current researched sixth-generation RAV4 GXL is **235/60R18**, so it must not inherit the older 225/60R18 budget.

## Fitment and evidence scope

- The previous anchors were premium retail choices, sometimes acoustic/EV or OE-marked. Those choices cannot be labelled the only compatible option.
- None of these prices includes a separately quoted wheel alignment. Do not silently treat supply-only prices as fitted. For example, [AC Tyres Transmate 99W](https://actyres.com.au/tyre-transmate-215-55r18-99w-sport-d1/) is $115 including GST, **fitting extra**, even though delivery to Brisbane workshops is free.
- Budget price evidence establishes an available cost tier, not equal safety, comfort, efficiency or wear performance. No comparative test was found in this audit that would justify equating these exact sizes/products with the earlier premium tyres on all those measures.
- Check a local fitted quote and actual placard before buying. This matters for Atto 3's changed wheel sizes across years/variants and any optional larger wheels.
