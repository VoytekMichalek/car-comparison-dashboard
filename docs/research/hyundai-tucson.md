## Specifications and ownership verification

Original 2021 and January 2023 Australian manufacturer tables agree on 2.0 MPi/115 kW/192 Nm, conventional six-speed automatic, FWD, 91 RON and E10 compatibility, ADR combined 8.1 / urban 11.0 L/100km, 54 L tank, 4,630 × 1,865 mm body, 539 L VDA upright boot and 1,650 kg braked towing with 100 kg towball limit. Base wheels are 235/65R17 104H. The manufacturer also establishes rear console vents, cloth upholstery, manual climate control and full-size spare. Base/Elite electronic equipment differs; the family review's generic equipment claims were not copied as base specifications. In particular, the base family article calls front seats power-adjustable, while the maker/launch-grade specification establishes manual adjustment.

ANCAP: five stars under the **2021** protocol, original NX4 Australian application covered, rating expiry **December 2027**. Its 87% child occupant result does not guarantee three specific child restraints fit.

The July 2021 manufacturer warranty passport gives five years/unlimited km for private use starting at first registration or first retail delivery, whichever is earlier. Prior commercial application, including rental/hire, can impose the five-year/130,000 km limit. Original 12 V battery coverage is two years/40,000 km; paint durability three years. Wear, maintenance and collision damage are separate. Detailed qualified-repairer invoices support original basic cover; current dealer-only conditions for the extra two years must not be backdated. The newer seven-year offer requires eligible first registration from 1 June 2025 and qualifying servicing.

Servicing: original exact base road test records 12 months/15,000 km and first-five services averaging A$319 each. A$1,595 is historical first-five pricing, not the cost of a used car's remaining ten years. Retain A$450/year (A$300–750 scenarios) as a longer-term allowance. Hyundai's current Genuine Service Plan stays with the eligible car and quotes a maximum for a stated effective period. The public Australian manual index fetched in this audit exposed electric manuals only; no original Australian NX4 2.0 maintenance table was located. ## Reviews and evidence quality

Existing professional results independently reread: CarsGuide original launch 7.8/10 and exact base family test 7.6/10. Both praise cabin/family practicality but identify modest naturally aspirated engine performance. Neither is a long-term reliability survey.

ProductReview's current NX4 page shows 2.5/5, 25 posts. It explicitly includes later hybrid and facelift posts, mixed 2.0 petrol/1.6 turbo/diesel grades and at least one independent expert's borrowed-car evaluation. Several original 2.0 posts discuss performance; later assistance-system or warranty complaints cannot all be assigned to the original base car. Self-selection, tiny matched subsamples and mixed generations within the NX4 label prevent a full-strength exact-variant owner score. Root has been told to apply the 0.25 scope factor.
