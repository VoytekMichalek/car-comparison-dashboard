## Matched Brisbane quotation-study cross-check

[Compare the Market, published 9 April 2026](https://www.comparethemarket.com.au/news/ev-insurance-up-in-12-months/) reports **Model Y RWD 3,203** and **EV5 Standard Range 1,921**, with cover commencing **5 March 2026**. It surveyed 11 insurers / six underwriters; not all products were available for every car, and exact per-model quote counts were not disclosed.

Profile: a 34-year-old male at Alderley 4051, clean history, garaged, private/commuting use up to 15,000 km/year, market value, targeted 900 excess or nearest available; full-time employment, no home ownership or other vehicle, white car with no options, modifications or damage. These are quoted prices for a fixed profile, not premiums paid by a representative Queensland cohort.

Keep the EV5 observation because it matches the selected Standard Range variant and Queensland location. Its national single-insurer mean is a useful separate check, not evidence that 1,921 is wrong. Do not pool the samples. The article contains **Zeekr X**, not 7X, so it cannot justify a 7X multiplier. It does not list Atto 3, EV6 or Ioniq 5.

## Remaining applicability gaps

- **Atto 3 EVO:** current range is predominantly evidence for the original nameplate. The dataset's independently sourced EVO launch review is dated 2 October 2026, after the insurer cohort ended; the old cohort cannot establish EVO's own premium. Essential is also not separated. Keep a plain provisional label and avoid describing four variants as independently quoted.
- **Model Y:** neither insurer aggregate nor the fixed-profile RWD study splits original versus Juniper or Long Range. No numerical premium-loading claim was established. Preserve the A$2,000 original-RWD default, and describe 2,260 elsewhere as a family proxy.
- **Ioniq 5:** no disclosed separation of 72.6/77.4/84-kWh batteries, Dynamiq/Techniq, AWD/RWD or used/new values. The mean is a family benchmark, not identical insurance costs proved across these cars.
- **EV6:** the published Air default draws from all EV6 policies, not a separately measured Air subgroup; no extra AWD/performance markup should be applied to this Air without evidence.
- **7X:** actual 7X range is materially better than an X-based proxy, but no separate Standard/Long Range sample or mean is published. The midpoint remains low confidence.

Insurance assumptions should remain yellow, editable and subordinate to an entered household quote. Actual price depends on address, drivers, claims, excess, insured value, parking, use and inclusions. Price differences across the observations cannot be assigned entirely to vehicle repair cost.

## Other evidence checked and not substituted

[RACQ's current published 2025 running-cost study](https://www.racq.com.au/voc) uses a different fixed driver profile: age 30, basic excess 850, rating 1 for life, no under-25 drivers, garaged at an average-risk postcode, RACQ Bank finance, market value, averaging male/female rates. Its table **combines registration and insurances**. Do not copy that combined column into comprehensive insurance or subtract today's registration without reconciling July 2025 charges and inclusions. Its residual values are August 2025, so it is not a fresh October 2026 insurance quote.

[CHOICE's April 2026 EV insurance research](https://www.choice.com.au/money/insurance/car/articles/the-cost-of-insuring-electric-vehicles) uses January 2026 quotes and reports powertrain-level averages. It supplies useful context but no reopened model-specific result for these six families. Applying its generic EV average on top of an existing nameplate benchmark would discard better specificity.

No additional current primary Queensland model-and-household quotation was established for Atto 3, EV6, Ioniq 5 or 7X. Forum anecdotes and generic broker ranges were not treated as sampled model averages.
