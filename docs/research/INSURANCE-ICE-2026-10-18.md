## Queensland profile study: useful, but keep the components separate

[RACQ's 2025 running-cost study](https://www.racq.com.au/car/research/car-running-costs/running-costs-2025) models new cars, five years and 15,000 km/year. Its comprehensive assumptions are age 30, averaged male/female premiums, A$850 excess, rating 1 for life, no under-25 drivers, average-risk postcode, garaged, private use, market value, RACQ Bank encumbrance. Statutory costs are dated **1 July 2025**. The displayed column **combines registration, CTP and comprehensive insurance**; it is not an insurance quote alone.

Relevant monthly combined costs: Outback AWD **A$174.15**, X-Trail ST 2WD **A$166.96**, Carnival S petrol **A$244.53**. The new Forester entry is **hybrid**, not the listed petrol configuration. These cannot be copied into comprehensive costs or combined with separate rego again. Do not subtract the app's current 2026 statutory estimate to infer a supposedly exact 2025 premium: that mixes dates and potentially CTP providers. The 2024 report also combines these components and is older.

- Every comprehensive point should visibly say **estimate** and expose insurer, cohort date, model scope and whether the anchor is a mean, median, midpoint or profile study.

- Current personalised QLD premiums, identical-cover cheapest-insurer comparisons and exact configuration-level samples remain gaps. The absence of such data does not justify a uniform SUV uplift.
