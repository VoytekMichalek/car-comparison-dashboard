## Warranty and safety conflicts that should remain visible

The April 2025 warranty announcement reported removal of several shorter component periods, including auxiliary battery exclusions. The **current 2026 handbook** nevertheless explicitly prints a 12-month/20,000 km lead-acid starting-battery term, plus 12-month emergency-cable terms and chargeable body adjustments. Treat the current booklet and written VIN confirmation as the useful buying evidence; do not retain an obsolete three-year infotainment limit as a reason to inflate the reserve.

The current booklet also excludes vehicles acquired at auction or imported other than by BYD Australia. It does not resolve this audit's question of how that clause applies to an ordinary used car with earlier auction history. Ask BYD for written remaining coverage if relevant; do not assert that any prior auction automatically cancels every subsequent buyer's rights. Australian Consumer Law rights are separate.

CarsGuide's EVO review contains contradictory embedded fields (seven-year unlimited warranty) alongside correct six-year narrative; it also carries original ANCAP stars without an explicit EVO extension. Those fields were not imported. Its charging-time statement uses a different window from the official brochure. Australian manufacturer numbers and ANCAP's explicit variant scope take precedence.
