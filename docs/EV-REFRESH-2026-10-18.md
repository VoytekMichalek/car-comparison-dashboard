## Material improvements

**MY23 Ioniq 5 Dynamiq now has a specific asking-price example.** [Private NSW advertisement SSE-AD-20746442](https://www.carsales.com.au/cars/details/2022-hyundai-ioniq-5-dynamiq-auto-2wd-my23/SSE-AD-20746442/) explicitly identifies a November 2022 build, NE.V3 MY23, 168 kW RWD, 77.4 kWh battery and 507 km rated range, with 42,904 km. The displayed ask is **A$41,900 excluding government charges**. The saved cohort is now one observation with low confidence, replacing the A$31,050 valuation point. The page was opened on 18 October, but its crawl metadata was about a month old; there was no sold, deposit or deadline wording. This is evidence of an advertised ask, not an independently confirmed available car or completed sale. Queensland transport/registration and the broader market remain unresolved. A VIN was not publicly displayed. Detail-page commentary was updated to match; a stale statement calling the price a guide proxy was removed.

**MY23 maintenance has separate obligations between general services.** The [Australian 2023 Ioniq 5 owner manual](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/au/en/owning/manuals/vehicle-manuals/ioniq-5/2023/IONIQ%205%202023%20Owner%20Manual.pdf), printed page **9-8**, specifies normal service at **24 months / 30,000 km**, low-conductivity coolant replacement every **36 months / 60,000 km**, and 12V battery inspection every **12 months / 15,000 km**, whichever comes first. The coolant can be replaced early with other maintenance, but a two-year general-service stamp alone does not prove the separate fluid obligation was satisfied. The 13.4 MB manufacturer PDF was downloaded and its actual maintenance table inspected after the web reader rejected its size. The generic search-generated table had misaligned rows and was not used. Both MY23 vehicle entries, inspection questions and ownership commentary now explain these intervals. No MY23 interval is extrapolated to the 84 kWh refresh or another country’s manual.

The existing A$375/year MY23 service estimate and sensitivity bounds remain estimates based on historical capped visits, with a stronger warning to establish whether coolant/off-cycle work is included. No current, model-specific Australian coolant tariff was verified. A generic engine-coolant-flush template and an isolated A$1,200 owner account were insufficient to manufacture a new exact charge or an expected annual failure cost.

**BYD major-service scope is clearer.** The [current Australian manufacturer service guide](https://bydautomotive.com.au/service-and-warranty) separately lists brake/drive-fluid work at **24 months / 40,000 km**, and coolant at **48 months / 80,000 km**, with drivetrain-specific additions. These generic-guide duties are now reflected in ownership commentary and inspection questions. The VIN’s original handbook and an itemised quote still govern; a minor-service starting price is not represented as every annual visit’s bill. No new model-specific tariff or retrospective handbook amendment was inferred.
